Item basics

Everything the calculator talks about, and where to find it in game.

Reading an item

Hover an item. First line: [Treasure][Weapon] = rarity + slot.

  • Main line, e.g. Attack Power +2,100. The white number is the item's own value (the calculator's “white number”).
  • Green +number after it = extra from enhancing. The +2 after the name is the enhance level.
  • [Bonus] lines, e.g. [Bonus]Skill Damage 24% = random extra stats (the calculator's “bonus lines”).

Tier

How strong the item is, 1 to 49. The tooltip doesn't show it: read it from the name. Iron = 4, Elite Iron = 5, Steel = 6, Elite Steel = 7, then a clan name + “Crest” for each pair (“Elite” = the higher one). Table below.

Only tier 4+ items get [Bonus] lines.

Rarity

[Common] white < [Rare] blue < [Treasure] pink < [Relic] red < [Divine] cyan.

Higher rarity = bigger main line and more [Bonus] lines (Common 0-1, Rare 0-2, Treasure 1-3, Relic 2-3, Divine 3; rings +1).

Enhancing

Right-click a Weapon, Armor or Helmet → Enhance. Each +1 adds +40% of its main line. Costs Currency (by tier), 35% success.

A fail cracks the item (can't enhance). Fix it: right-click → Use Meteoric Iron, max 5 times per item. The weapon is the best one to enhance.

What you can wear

You can wear the tier that the highest stage or boss you cleared solo drops (tiers 1-3 always). Too high = red “Equip Restriction”. Party clears don't count.

Check with -1: “Highest Cleared Training Ground” and “Highest cleared Boss Battle”.

Where gear drops

  • Training stage: only its Hunting Ground Boss drops gear (1/30 at 0 Condition).
  • Boss Battle: always a Common, Rare and Treasure item; Relic 20%, Divine 6.25%. First 2 boss kills each day: triple chances.
  • Item Shop “Drop Purchase”: the same roll as a boss kill for Currency, bosses you cleared, 10 per 10 h.
  • Boss Sweep: 1 Boss Ticket, half the drop chances.

Attack Power

The base of all your damage = hero base + Training + gear. Early, gear is most of it, so Attack Power lines and weapon enhancing are huge. Later, Training grows far past gear.

Bound items

Most drops bind to the character. About 10% drop Unbound: right-click → share to move them to another character. Ghost Iron removes a binding.

Raids (Vajra Body & Yin-Yang Body)

What you get

Each raid reward: 1 Money Bag + 1 Boss Ticket + 5 Essence Fragments, plus a small chance at an Essence (the raid gear).

  • Yin-Yang Essence (Special slot): multiplies your Basic and Skill Damage % by +15% (Lv 1) up to +55% (Lv 11). The big damage item.
  • Vajra Essence (Special slot): multiplies HP and HP Regen by +15% up to +71% (Lv 15).

You can wear both at once.

How to raid

  • Side menu → Raid button teleports you to the arena. Talk to Vajra Body or Yin-Yang Body and pick a difficulty (Vajra 1-15, Yin-Yang 1-11).
  • No unlock needed solo: any difficulty you can beat.
  • Kill 10 melee + 3 ranged + the boss within 100 s. Fail = no reward, no other penalty.
  • Set your gear first: you can't swap gear during a raid.

Limits & shortcuts

  • 3 rewards per raid type every 5 h of playtime (starts at your first reward). -hour shows “Vajra x/3” and the time left.
  • Clear a difficulty solo once and you can claim it (and anything lower) instantly afterwards, no fight.
  • Party (up to 6): each player must have cleared the previous difficulty solo. Party clears don't count as your own clear.

Essences

  • An Essence of level n only drops from difficulty n. Chance: 0.8% × your Drop bonus, rising a little after each miss (-probability shows it).
  • You can't level an Essence up: a higher one replaces it.
  • Enhance: right-click → Enhance. Currency + 1 Fragment per try, 5% success, never breaks. About 20 Fragments (4 rewards) per +1.

Order to do them

  • Start with Vajra 1 (easiest) and push Vajra solo.
  • Yin-Yang 1 is as hard as Vajra 5. Once you can do it, take all 3 Yin-Yang rewards every 5 h first: it's the damage one.
  • Open Money Bags before leaving: they are not saved (each = 3× your best Training Ground's Currency).

How it works

Skill hit = Attack Power × skill number × (1 + Skill Damage) × (1 + All Damage) × skill crit
Normal attack = Attack Power × (1 + Basic Attack Damage) × (1 + All Damage) × basic crit
  • Everything multiplies. +20% on a stat you have little of is worth far more than +20% on a stat you already stacked. That is why the list is different for every character.
  • Your hero's own bonus and your gear's bonus multiply too. For % stats only your gear total of that stat decides how good the next line is.
  • Attack Power = hero base + Training + gear. Early on gear is most of it, so Attack Power lines are huge. As your Training grows, Attack Power lines become weak and % lines take over.
  • Crit just adds up (every hero starts at 1% chance, +50% damage). Some heroes get a lot from their kit (Fist Fighter, Archer), so crit lines help them less.
  • Procs and skills marked “counts as basic attack” use Basic and Skill Damage and crit with skill crit. So Skill Damage lines also help basic heroes.
  • Attack Speed helps normal attacks and things that trigger on attack. With the in-game Auto skill button on, a skill that is off cooldown waits for your next hit before it casts, so Attack Speed also makes skills come out a bit sooner. Short-cooldown skills lose the most (a 2 s skill on a slow hero casts about 25% less often). Using Auto skills? Tick ⚙️ Settings → I use Auto skills.
❤️ Survival (dying a lot?)
By default everything is judged on damage. If a character dies in its farm zone or can't finish a boss, open ⚙️ Settings → Survival:
  • Some: HP counts a little (1% more HP ≈ ¼ of 1% more damage).
  • A lot: HP counts more (1% more HP ≈ ½ of 1% more damage).
  • Heavy: 1% more HP ≈ ¾ of 1% more damage.
  • Equal (1:1): 1% more HP counts the same as 1% more damage. Only if survival is your real wall.
  • Custom: type your own number, 0 to 5. It is how many % damage 1% more HP is worth (2 = HP counts double).
It then also suggests HP / HP Regen upgrades and Trade items that lose a little damage for a lot of HP. The “+x% HP” is measured against your character's real total HP, so early armor upgrades show big numbers and late ones small.
HP Regen counts half as much as HP (it helps over time, not against big hits). Blood Cult counts it the same as HP, since Regen refills what its skills spend. Change it under Settings → HP Regen vs HP (shows when Survival is on).
Blood Cult benefits most: its skills cost its own HP, and Bloodburst switches off below 20% HP.
Not dying? Leave it Off. Extra HP doesn't make you kill faster.
